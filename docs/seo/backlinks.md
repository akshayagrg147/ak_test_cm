## Ground rules

- **Be truthful.** ViaCRM has no paying customers yet; the STKT workspace is a
  trial. Do not claim customer counts, ratings, awards or "trusted by"
  statements. Do not post or buy reviews. Ask real users for reviews only once
  they exist.
- **No WhatsApp claims.** WhatsApp is not a ViaCRM feature.
- **No paid link schemes.** Skip "submit to 200 directories for $99" services,
  PBNs, link exchanges and paid guest posts. They risk a Google penalty.
  Directory PPC and sponsored placements are advertising, not SEO. If you buy
  them, the links should be `nofollow`/`sponsored`.
- **Keep the details identical everywhere.** Use the same name, URL, logo and
  description (NAP consistency):
  - Name: **ViaCRM**
  - Website: **https://viacrm.in/** (no `www`)
  - Category: CRM software › Real estate CRM
  - Logo: `https://viacrm.in/logo-512.png`. The share image is
    `https://viacrm.in/og-image.png` (1200×630).
  - Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/viacrm91/
- **Use the right landing page.** Link to `https://viacrm.in/real-estate-crm/`
  when a site allows a "product URL" separate from the homepage. Link to the
  homepage otherwise.
- **DA** below is an approximate Moz Domain Authority / Ahrefs DR range from
  public directory lists. Treat it as a rough guide, not a measured value.
  Several lists disagree by 5 to 10 points.

## Reusable copy

### One-liner (≤ 60 chars)

> Real estate CRM for Indian brokers and agencies

### Short description (≈ 160 chars)

> ViaCRM is a real estate CRM for Indian brokers and agencies: lead capture,
> telecalling with call recordings, site visits, inventory and bookings in one
> place.

### Medium description (≈ 100 words)

> ViaCRM is a real estate CRM built for Indian property dealers, brokers,
> agencies and channel partners. It captures enquiries from website forms,
> Google Ads lead forms, IndiaMART and Justdial, and assigns each lead to a team
> member. Telecallers get focused calling queues, follow-up dates and alerts for
> overdue call-backs. The ViaCRM Android app links calls, and the recordings
> made by the phone's built-in recorder, to the right lead. Teams can manage
> property inventory, schedule site visits, hand leads to field executives, and
> track bookings and commissions. Each company gets its own branding and
> role-based access. Data is hosted on AWS in Mumbai.

### Long description (≈ 200 words, for G2/Capterra/SaaSworthy "About")

> ViaCRM is a real estate CRM for Indian brokers, agencies, channel partners and
> developers' sales teams. Most property deals are lost in the gaps: a
> call-back nobody made, a site visit that was not confirmed, a buyer's budget
> written in a diary. ViaCRM closes those gaps by keeping each lead, its owner
> and its next step in one shared workspace.
>
> **Lead capture:** website enquiry forms, Google Ads lead forms, IndiaMART
> polling, Justdial webhooks and CSV import with duplicate detection.
>
> **Telecalling:** assigned queues, call outcomes, follow-up dates and an
> automatic bell for overdue call-backs. The Android app syncs calls and the
> phone's built-in call recordings to the matching lead. Support depends on the
> device.
>
> **Real estate workflow:** sale and rental inventory with photos and
> brochures, buyer budget and location matching, site visits with field
> executive handoffs, and bookings with commissions.
>
> **For the business:** role-based access for admins, telecallers and field
> executives; dashboards with charts; attendance, leave and payroll; and
> company-level branding.
>
> Data is hosted in India on AWS Mumbai. Support is available in English and
> Hindi.

## Not recommended

- Bulk directory submission services (SoftwareSuggest "ListingSaaS",
  Fiverr "500 backlinks", "DA 50+ guest posts" sellers). These are paid links,
  low quality and risky.
- Article spinning and web 2.0 blogs, comment links, forum signatures.
- Fake reviews on G2/Capterra/Google. They violate those sites' policies and
  Indian consumer-protection guidance on fake online reviews.
- Link exchanges with unrelated sites.
