What good lead management looks like

1. Every lead has an owner

Assign leads to telecallers or agents. Each person sees their own queue; admins see everything.

2. Every lead has a next step and a date

Calls end with an outcome and a follow-up. Overdue call-backs show in a bell that stays until a result is saved or the call-back is rescheduled.

3. Closed and DND leads stay out of the way

Priority lists exclude closed, do-not-disturb and archived clients, so the team only works leads that can still convert.

4. Managers see the funnel

Admin and telecaller dashboards chart calls, follow-ups and pipeline by person and time period, with exportable reports.

Built for real estate, not generic sales

Leads carry budget, preferred locations and property type, and can be matched with available inventory. A lead can move into a site visit, a field executive handoff and finally a booking — without leaving its history behind.

Frequently asked questions

What is lead management software?

Lead management software collects enquiries from all your sources, assigns each one to a person, and tracks every call and follow-up until the lead converts or closes.

Can ViaCRM import leads from IndiaMART and Justdial?

Yes. IndiaMART leads are pulled on a schedule using your IndiaMART CRM key, and Justdial leads arrive by webhook.

Can I import my existing Excel leads?

Yes. Save the sheet as CSV and use the import tool; it maps columns and reports duplicates before you confirm.
