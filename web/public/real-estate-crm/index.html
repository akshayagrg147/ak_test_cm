Why brokers move from Excel and diaries to a real estate CRM

Most agencies lose deals in the gaps: a call-back that nobody made, a site visit that was not confirmed, a buyer whose budget was written on paper. A real estate CRM closes those gaps by putting each lead, its owner and its next step in one place that the whole team can see.

How a lead moves through ViaCRM

  1. Capture: the enquiry arrives from your form, ad or portal integration and is assigned to a team member.
  2. Qualify: the telecaller records budget, preferred locations and property type, and sets the next follow-up.
  3. Visit: a site visit is scheduled and, if needed, handed to a field executive who accepts it.
  4. Close: the booking, agreed value and commission are recorded against the same customer history.

Built for teams, with the right access for each role

Admins see the whole company. Telecallers see the leads assigned to them. Field executives see their current handoffs and their own work. Recordings cannot be deleted by callers or executives, and every company's data stays separate. Dashboards with charts show calls, follow-ups and pipeline by person and period.

Your company, your brand

Each company workspace carries its own logo and brand, its own lead stages, custom fields and roles. Attendance, leave approvals and payroll are available for teams that want HR basics in the same place.

Data is hosted on AWS in Mumbai (ap-south-1). ViaCRM does not include WhatsApp messaging.

Frequently asked questions

What is a real estate CRM?

A real estate CRM is software that stores every property enquiry with its source, owner, budget, preferred locations and next step, so brokers and agencies can follow up on time, schedule site visits and track bookings.

Does ViaCRM work for small brokers as well as agencies?

Yes. A single broker can use it to keep follow-ups on time, and agencies can add telecallers and field executives with role-based access.

Which lead sources can ViaCRM capture?

Website enquiry forms, Google Ads lead forms, IndiaMART and Justdial integrations, and CSV/Excel-exported files through the import tool.

Does ViaCRM record calls?

The ViaCRM Android app uses the recordings your phone already makes with its built-in call recorder, after you give it access to that folder, and attaches them to the matching lead. Recording availability depends on the phone model and Android version.

Where is my data stored?

ViaCRM runs on AWS in the Mumbai region, and each company's data is kept separate.
