How to follow up real estate leads so none go cold

Most property leads are not lost to a better offer. They are lost because nobody called back. Here is a simple routine any broker or agency can run.

Published 14 November 2026 · ViaCRM

1. Call new enquiries the same day

A buyer who fills a form or calls from a portal listing is usually talking to several brokers. The first useful conversation wins attention. Set a rule: every new enquiry gets a call the same working day, and ideally within the hour.

2. Ask the same five questions every time

  • Buying or renting? For self-use or investment?
  • Budget range, and is a home loan involved?
  • Preferred locations or projects.
  • Property type and size (2 BHK, plot, shop…).
  • When do they want to move or decide?

Write the answers in the lead record, not in a notebook. The next person who calls should not ask again.

3. End every call with a date

"I'll call you later" is not a follow-up. Agree a day and time, and save it. If the buyer is not ready, a follow-up in two weeks is still a follow-up.

4. Move to a site visit quickly

Once budget and location fit, offer two visit slots. Confirm the day before. After the visit, record feedback the same day: what they liked, objections and the next step.

5. Review overdue follow-ups daily

A manager should spend ten minutes each morning on overdue call-backs: who missed them and why. This is where a CRM helps most — ViaCRM shows overdue call-backs in a bell that stays until a result is saved.

6. Close leads honestly

Mark leads as closed, lost or do-not-disturb when that is the truth. A clean list means your team spends time on buyers who can still convert.

A simple follow-up schedule

WhenAction
Day 0First call, qualify with the five questions, share 2–3 matching options.
Day 1–2Follow-up call; offer site visit slots.
Day 3–7Site visit; record feedback the same day.
Day 7–14Negotiation or new options based on feedback.
MonthlyNurture call for buyers who are not ready yet.
