Telecaller CRM with call recordings linked to every lead

The ViaCRM Android app matches calls from your team's phones to the right lead and uploads the phone's own call recordings, so managers can review conversations without asking for screenshots.

How call sync works

  1. Your telecaller signs in to the ViaCRM Android app with their CRM account.
  2. They see the leads assigned to them and call from the phone as usual.
  3. After they allow it, the app reads the call log for those calls and the recordings folder they choose.
  4. Each recording is matched to the closest eligible call by time and uploaded to the lead's history.
The app uses recordings made by your phone's built-in call recorder. It does not record calls itself, and availability depends on the phone brand, model and Android version. We will tell you honestly if a device does not support it.

What managers get

Call history per lead

Every synced call and recording appears in the lead's timeline next to remarks and follow-ups.

Hourly call charts

See when your team is calling and how many calls each person made in a period.

Recordings that stay

Telecallers and field executives cannot delete recordings. Access follows the lead's permissions.

What telecallers get

A home screen with today's work, their assigned leads, call history and a read-only property catalogue to answer buyer questions during the call.

Frequently asked questions

Does ViaCRM record calls itself?

No. It uses the recordings your phone's built-in call recorder already saves, after you give the app access to that folder.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones whose built-in dialer can save call recordings. Support varies by brand, model and Android version, so test with your team's phones first.

Can a telecaller delete a recording?

No. Telecallers and field executives have no recording-delete action.
