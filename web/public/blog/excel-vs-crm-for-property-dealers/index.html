Excel vs CRM for property dealers: when to switch

Spreadsheets are free and familiar. For a broker with a handful of buyers they are enough. Here is how to tell when they start costing you deals.

Published 14 November 2026 · ViaCRM

Signs you have outgrown Excel

  • Two people called the same buyer, or nobody did.
  • You cannot see which follow-ups were due yesterday.
  • Leads arrive from IndiaMART, Justdial, ads and walk-ins, and someone copies them by hand.
  • The sheet has many versions on different phones.
  • You cannot tell which telecaller made how many calls this week.

Excel vs a real estate CRM

Excel / Google SheetsReal estate CRM
Lead captureManual copy-pasteForms, ads and portal integrations
Follow-up remindersNone, unless you build themAutomatic overdue alerts
Team accessEveryone sees everythingRole-based: each person sees their leads
Call historyA notes columnTimeline with calls, remarks and recordings
Site visits and bookingsSeparate sheetsLinked to the same customer
ReportsBuilt by handDashboards by person and period

What to look for in a CRM for property dealers

  1. Real estate fields: budget, location, property type and inventory matching.
  2. Your lead sources: check that the portals and ad forms you use are supported.
  3. A telecaller workflow: queues, outcomes and follow-up dates, not just a contact list.
  4. Mobile: an Android app for calling teams.
  5. Data location and access control: who can see, export or delete what.
  6. Easy import: bring your existing sheet in without retyping.
